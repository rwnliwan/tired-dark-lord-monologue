**The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue**.

## The entry

A fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — but the tiredness never
gets said out loud. It lives in the voice, the rhythm, and the small
deflections: the bards remember only the cape, every hero arrives with the
same speech, the scheme is fine, the volcano is ready, the army is fed, the
ravens have memorised the route. What tires him is the speeches.

The monologue is 95 words / 517 characters, inside the gate of 1–600
characters for `/output/entry.txt`.

> I have conquered seven kingdoms, and the bards remember only the cape. Four hundred years, and every hero arrives with the same speech about light, sword shaking, voice breaking on the word 'destiny'. I killed him Tuesday. He came back Thursday with the same speech. The scheme is fine. The volcano is ready, the army is fed, and the ravens have memorised the route. It is the speeches that tire me. So tonight, hero — sit down. Say the speech. I have heard it eleven times, and I will tell you exactly where it ends.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` when set) and prints its word and character counts.

```sh
node main.ts
```
